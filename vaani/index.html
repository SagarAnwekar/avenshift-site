Honest notes

What this demo is.
And what it is not.

A real demo, a small one.

This page understands simple sale sentences in English, Hindi and Hinglish, and does the addition itself. It can mishear or mis-add. Always check the numbers before trusting them.

Your words stay with you.

Listening is done by your browser's speech service (on Chrome, that is Google's speech recognizer). This page itself saves nothing and sends no speech or sales text, except audio when you tap the optional Deepgram button. The full product will say clearly where data goes before it asks for any.

Voices depend on your device.

The spoken reply uses the voices already on your phone or computer. Many devices have a Hindi voice; fewer have Marathi. If yours does not, the reply still plays in the closest voice available.

The product is in development.

Vaani is being built by Avenshift for shop owners who would rather speak than type. This demo shows the idea; the full product, with saved history and better understanding, is not live yet.
