01 / The proposed workflow

From a contact sheet
to a callback report.

Our proposed workflow connects contact sheets, permission-based calls, course and webinar questions, transcripts, WhatsApp follow-ups and callback reports.

01 / INPUT

Contact sheet

Name and phone number.

02 / CONVERSATION

Permission-based call

Course and webinar questions. Understand the requirement.

03 / FOLLOW-UP

Transcript & WhatsApp

Review the conversation. Share the right details.

04 / REPORT

Callback report

Interested people and next steps.

IN DEVELOPMENT / Not yet a live customer service.
02 / The design principles

Built for useful answers,
not guesses.

Calling and WhatsApp setup must be checked before a pilot.

Use approved information.

Escalate unanswered questions to a person.

Make the AI clear.

Disclose AI and recording. Respect opt-outs.
